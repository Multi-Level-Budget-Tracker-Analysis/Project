MULTI-LEVEL BUDGET TRACKER ANALYTICS

A unified, full-stack financial intelligence platform and Power BI enterprise architecture analyzing economic dynamics across three hierarchical tiers: Micro (Personal Household Budgets), Meso (Enterprise Departmental Cost Centers), and Macro (State Public Finance & National Union Budgets). Designed with live user input studios, real-time recalculations, Holt-Winters predictive forecasting, and verified against authentic Kaggle and RBI data series.

⚡ Live User Input Studios 👤 10-Category Spend Analysis 🏢 Department Cost vs Revenue 🇮🇳 15+ State Public Deficits 🔮 95% Confidence Forecasts 📊 Star Schema Power BI Hub

Tier 1 • Micro-Budget

Individual Mode

Manage personal and household finances. Input your monthly salary, secondary income, and custom spending targets across 10 essential categories. Dynamically compute your savings rate %, detect overspending, and track your financial health score.

  • 10-Category spend & target budget studio
  • Real-time Donut Chart & Variance color-coded bar chart
  • 12-Month simulated trajectory vs. budget threshold
  • Financial Health Scorecard (0-100 Rating)
  • Searchable personal transactions ledger with CRUD
🏢
Tier 2 • Meso-Budget

Organization Mode

Analyze enterprise financial health using Superstore cost center modeling. Enter departmental operational budgets, monitor operating costs (Cost = Sales - Profit), evaluate gross profit margins, and inspect regional performance matrices.

  • Corporate, Consumer & Home Office department modeling
  • Clustered Bar Chart: Revenue vs. Cost comparison
  • Quarterly revenue growth trajectory (% YoY)
  • Profitability treemap drill-down by product category
  • Region × Category variance heatmap matrix
🇮🇳
Tier 3 • Macro-Budget

State & Nation Mode

Explore macroeconomic public finance data from RBI and Government of India Union Budgets. Investigate state revenues, expenditures, fiscal deficits, debt-to-revenue ratios, and national central sector allocations across key ministries.

  • 15+ Indian State public finance interactive cards
  • Multi-year state revenue vs. expenditure line chart
  • National Union Budget sector allocation ranking
  • Debt servicing ratio & deficit tracking
  • Authentic Kaggle & RBI statistical series
